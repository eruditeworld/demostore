How It Works

Whether you are ordering fresh organic henna cones or enrolling in online masterclasses, our process is straightforward, secure, and personally handled.

Product Orders

Ordering Organic Henna

PAN India courier dispatch right to your doorstep.

1

Browse Products

Explore our organic cones, triple-sifted powders, natural oils, and bridal packs in the Shop.

2

Choose Your Product

Select your required quantities on the product page and click Buy Now to start checkout.

3

Enter Delivery Details

Fill in your full name, 10-digit mobile and WhatsApp number, and complete delivery address with 6-digit pincode.

4

Pay via UPI

Tap the dynamic UPI link on your mobile to pay via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or scan the generated QR code.

5

Confirm on WhatsApp

Click Payment Completed — Confirm on WhatsApp to instantly send your pre-formatted order summary.

6

Order Verification & Processing

Nabila Khan verifies your transaction, packs your fresh batch, and shares your express tracking details on WhatsApp.
