Organic Henna Products & Shipping

Are the henna products organic?

Yes, absolutely. Our henna is crafted exclusively from triple-sifted natural Lawsonia Inermis leaves sourced from certified farms in Sojat, Rajasthan. We blend our paste solely with pure essential oils (Eucalyptus, Clove Bud, Cajeput), natural sugar, and lemon juice. We never use chemical dyes, PPD, ammonia, or synthetic shelf-life enhancers.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, we provide PAN India delivery to all pin codes serviced by reliable express courier partners. Cones are securely packed with tip seals and insulated wrapping to maintain peak dye release and freshness during transit.

How do I place an order?

Select your desired products in the Shop, choose your quantity, and click "Buy Now". Enter your shipping address, complete your direct UPI payment, and click "Confirm on WhatsApp" to send your details directly to Nabila Khan.

Which UPI apps can I use?

You can pay using any standard Indian UPI application including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED, or mobile banking apps. On smartphones, clicking the "Pay via UPI" button automatically opens your installed app with the exact amount and Order ID pre-filled.

How do I confirm my payment?

After completing your transaction in your UPI app, return to our website and click the green button titled "Payment Completed — Confirm on WhatsApp". This generates an encoded WhatsApp message with your Order ID, product details, and address for Nabila Khan to confirm.

How will my order be confirmed?

Nabila Khan manually cross-references your transaction against her UPI merchant account upon receiving your WhatsApp message. Once verified, she sends an immediate acknowledgment and later dispatches your tracking link as soon as the courier parcel is handed over.

Can I contact Nabila before ordering?

Certainly! You can click the floating WhatsApp button on any page or call 8447783091 if you have questions regarding batch dates, bulk requirements, or ingredient sensitivity.

Online Mehndi Academy & Courses

Are the courses beginner-friendly?

Yes! Our Beginner Mehndi Course starts from the absolute foundation: proper hand posture, relaxing finger muscles, controlling cone flow, and mastering clean teardrops, lines, hums, and swirls before moving on to fuller designs.

How do online courses work?

Our courses consist of structured, step-by-step video demonstrations accompanied by printable practice grid sheets. You can practice at your own pace from home. As you finish each module, you submit photos of your work to Nabila on WhatsApp for direct feedback.

How do I enroll in a course?

Visit the Courses page, choose the curriculum fitting your current skill level, and click "Enroll Now". Enter your student details, complete the course fee payment via UPI, and click "Confirm Enrollment on WhatsApp" to receive your onboarding kit.

How will I receive course access?

Once you confirm your payment on WhatsApp, Nabila Khan verifies your enrollment and provides direct links to your video modules, syllabus guide, and practice materials on WhatsApp.

Are live classes available?

The primary core modules are pre-recorded in crisp high definition so you can zoom in, pause, rewind, and practice whenever convenient. Nabila conducts periodic live Q&A sessions and review check-ins for active batches.

Will course delivery methods change in the future?

Yes. While WhatsApp is currently our primary personalized channel for direct mentorship and module access, our architecture is designed to expand into student portals, dedicated YouTube unlisted playlists, and Zoom workshop integrations in future updates.
