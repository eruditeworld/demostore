The Artisan & Educator

Meet Nabila Khan

Nabila Khan combines her deep passion for traditional Mehndi artistry with modern education and meticulously crafted henna products. Believing that real beauty springs from nature, she crafts every batch of henna paste using only certified organic botanicals.

As an educator, Nabila demystifies the craft: breaking down intricate bridal patterns into gentle, understandable techniques so every student can gain confidence, hone their hand stability, and turn their creative love for Mehndi into expressive art.

Product Philosophy

Triple-sifted Sojat henna, zero chemical accelerants (no PPD or synthetic dye), and pure therapeutic essential oils for safe, dark natural stains.

Teaching Philosophy

Structured, self-paced step-by-step guidance paired with honest 1-on-1 feedback on WhatsApp to nurture genuine artistic independence.
